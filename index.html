Bindu R

Founder

Bindu R is a 21-year-old entrepreneur and technology founder, currently pursuing a B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Science with a minor in Entrepreneurship at RV University, Bengaluru. She is the Founder of BR Innovation & Technology, focused on building scalable, human-centric products at the intersection of AI, education, and emotional learning.

Inspired by growing up in a family-run MSME business, Bindu witnessed how strong capability can be held back by language barriers. This personal experience shaped her mission to build solutions that empower confidence and communication from an early age.

Through BR Innovation & Technology, she is developing SakhiBot, an AI-powered spoken-English companion designed to help children build communication confidence in a safe, judgment-free environment. In parallel, she has led the development of Emova, an IoT-enabled emotional support plush toy, and ComfyCub, a sustainable babywear brand—demonstrating her ability to execute across hardware, software, consumer products, and brand building.

With a hands-on, execution-first approach and the ability to work across diverse user groups through her multilingual background (5+ languages), Bindu combines empathy, discipline, and technology to build products with long-term impact.

Her vision is to create globally relevant solutions that give the next generation confidence, voice, and equal access to opportunity—starting at the earliest stages of learning.
